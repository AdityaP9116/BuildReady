# WebMCP & Autonomous Agents — Project Hub

> Exported from [the canonical Notion page](https://app.notion.com/p/3cbf393aa76881f18e5ac14ed6e6f8af) on 2026-08-30. This repository copy is a point-in-time snapshot.

> **Purpose:** Build an evidence-backed project and idea-generation hub for WebMCP, MCP, AI agents, agent security, and the emerging agentic web.
## Project thesis
WebMCP can become the site-controlled action layer of the agentic web: web applications expose typed, browser-native capabilities that agents can invoke more reliably than clicking through interfaces. The most valuable opportunities will emerge where authenticated browser state, multi-step workflows, and explicit authorization meet.
## Project goals
- Track the WebMCP specification, browser implementation status, tooling, and ecosystem.
- Understand the broader MCP, A2A, agent-commerce, and agent-runtime landscape.
- Identify markets receiving the greatest capital, research effort, and enterprise adoption.
- Convert research into specific, defensible hackathon and startup concepts.
- Maintain a clear boundary between verified findings, emerging hypotheses, and unverified market claims.
## Current opportunity focus
1. Agent-readiness tooling for existing websites
2. WebMCP security, identity, and delegated authorization
3. Evaluation and observability for browser-native tools
4. Hybrid runtimes combining WebMCP, MCP, DOM/accessibility, and computer use
5. Vertical workflows in enterprise SaaS, commerce, customer operations, healthcare administration, finance, legal, and cybersecurity
## Project navigation
The detailed research page contains the consolidated landscape, market map, security analysis, open questions, and idea backlog.
## Working principles
- Prefer primary sources and executable benchmarks.
- Treat funding numbers and market forecasts as directional unless independently corroborated.
- Separate a logged-in session from permission to perform a consequential action.
- Design for visible user control, reversible actions, auditability, and least privilege.
- Revisit conclusions as the WebMCP draft and browser implementations evolve.
